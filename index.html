VARTA

Zero-overhead health protocol for distributed local agents.

32-byte heartbeats over Unix Domain Sockets. No dependencies. No allocations. Sub-microsecond.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect

    Varta::connect() opens a non-blocking Unix Datagram socket. One allocation. That's it.

  2. 2

    Beat

    agent.beat(Status::Ok, payload) encodes 32 bytes on the stack and calls send(2). Returns Sent, Dropped, or Failed.

  3. 3

    Observe

    varta-watch polls the socket, tracks per-pid state machines, detects stalls, runs recovery commands, exports Prometheus metrics.
